Set up Sidea on your Mac

If you’ve been invited to try Sidea, follow these three steps. Use made-up examples only.

First, check your Mac

Open the Apple menu and choose About This Mac. Look for an Apple chip (such as M1 or M2) and macOS 26.2 or later. Have about 10 GB free for the app and its first setup.

  1. Download Sidea. Sign in to GitHub with the account you were invited to use, then come back here to download the app.
    Download Sidea for Mac ↗
  2. Move Sidea to Applications. If Sidea is already open, quit it first. Open the downloaded file and drag Sidea Preview onto Applications. Then open Sidea Preview from Applications. Help if your Mac won’t open Sidea
  3. Finish setup inside Sidea. Choose Download local model. It needs an internet connection to download about 6.8 GB the first time. When the download finishes, choose Unlock Sidea.

How can we help?

Find answers about downloading, opening, and trying Sidea.

My Mac won’t open Sidea

Apple hasn’t verified this preview. If your Mac says it can’t verify Sidea and you trust the download, choose Done. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, then select Open Anyway and confirm. If your work Mac blocks this option, ask your IT team. If the warning says Sidea is damaged or contains malware, stop and contact whoever invited you.

Apple’s guide with screenshots ↗

I can’t download the app

Make sure you’re signed in to GitHub with the account that received the invitation. You can also open the download page on GitHub and choose Sidea-Preview-arm64.dmg under Assets. If you still can’t open it, check with the person who invited you.

Do my notes leave my Mac?

Sidea stores and searches your notes on your Mac. Setup uses the internet to download the AI model. If you create a backup or export, you choose where to save that copy.

What should I use for a first try?

Make up a patient and a short note, such as a reminder to check how a new routine is going. Then ask Sidea to help you prepare for that patient’s next visit. HIPAA compliance hasn’t been established for this preview.

What about Windows and Linux?

Sidea is available for Mac today. Tell us what you use so we can see which platform people want next. Please leave patient information out of public requests.
